The defaults
------------

The defaults of ``determine_periods``, ``process_vorticity`` and
``get_periods`` are the calibration preset **params-track** (below), with one
exception: ``boundary_padding`` defaults to ``"reflect"``, while params-track
uses ``"edge"``. They changed with respect to the 2.0.0 release; the
:repo:`CHANGELOG.md` lists every change.

(``docs/figures/make_doc_tables.py`` in the repository).

What was calibrated
-------------------

* **Phase detection.** The phase-detection defaults were calibrated against
  manually labelled phase sequences of real and synthetic cyclones. One rule,
  ``incipient_plateau_spare_intensification=True``, was adopted without an
  independent validation.
* **Filtering.** Only the filtering of **tracking series** was calibrated:
  hourly 850 hPa relative vorticity along South-Atlantic cyclone tracks, as a
  tracking algorithm delivers it. It was calibrated with
  ``boundary_padding="edge"``; the default ``"reflect"`` was not part of that
  calibration.

params-track: the measured preset
---------------------------------

params-track is the configuration whose results were measured against the
manual labels, and it is the only one. Those measurements were made with its
own ``boundary_padding="edge"``. They describe params-track, **not** the
package defaults, which have no measured scores of their own.

.. csv-table:: params-track against the package defaults (generated by ``docs/figures/make_doc_tables.py``)
   :file: generated/params_track_vs_defaults.csv
   :header-rows: 1

``use_filter: True`` in the preset is treated by the package as ``'auto'`` (with
a warning), so in practice only ``boundary_padding`` differs.

To run params-track, download
:repo:`research/labels/configs/cyclophaser_params-track.yaml` and pass its two
sections:

.. code-block:: python

   import yaml
   from cyclophaser import determine_periods

   with open("cyclophaser_params-track.yaml") as f:
       preset = yaml.safe_load(f)

   result = determine_periods(series, **preset["filter_params"], **preset["phase_params"])

Other inputs
------------

Other inputs were **not** calibrated: other levels or variables, other time
steps, gridded fields, or series that were not filtered by a tracking
algorithm.

Some parameters are counted in **time steps**, so their meaning depends on the
sampling interval of the series:

* ``cutoff_low`` and ``cutoff_high``;
* ``use_filter``, ``use_smoothing`` and ``use_smoothing_twice``, when given as a
  window length (an integer);
* ``replace_endpoints_with_lowpass``;
* ``incipient_smooth_window`` and ``incipient_plateau_k``.

The duration thresholds (``threshold_*``) are fractions of a length, not counts
of time steps.
