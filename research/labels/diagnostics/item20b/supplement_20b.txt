(1) DC CONTENT OF df['z'] (= vorticity_smoothed2 = the Lanczos output)
==============================================================================
cyclophaser/lanczos_filter.py documents this directly: at
window_length_lanczo = len(series)//2 the band-pass kernel does NOT
reject DC - sum(weights) has a documented median of 0.629. So the
filtered series keeps most of the raw mean rather than losing it.

effective DC gain  mean(z)/mean(zeta), over the 35 real train series:
   min 0.2408   median 0.6542   max 0.8343
   -> the filtered series is NOT zero-mean; it keeps 65% of the raw mean (median)

|mean(z)| / (z_max - z_min), same 35 series:
   min 0.36   median 0.93   max 2.81
   -> the offset is 0.9x the series' own peak-to-peak range (median): z sits well below zero and oscillates about that offset.

spread of the gain across series: max-min = 0.5935
   the gain depends on window_length_lanczo = len(series)//2, so it is
   SERIES-DEPENDENT: the baseline D2 measures |z| against is a different
   fraction of the physical zero in each series.
