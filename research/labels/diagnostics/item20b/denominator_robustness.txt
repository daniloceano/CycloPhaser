DENOMINATOR ROBUSTNESS - is D1 hostage to a single point (z_max)?
==============================================================================
D1 = (z_max - z_valley) / (z_max - z_min). z_max is one sample. If it is
an isolated spike, every D1 in that series is governed by it.

exactly - they ARE the series minimum, so their D1 is 1 for ANY finite
denominator. The denominator can only change a verdict for valleys near
the floor. Those are enumerated below, with D1 recomputed using the 2nd
